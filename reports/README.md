# Submission materials — deferred

The separate two-page business/technical summary, 6–10 page report, 5–7 slide
presentation, and reviewed data-specific one-page planner view are intentionally not
generated on this experiment branch. They must be built only after the full study has
completed and its aggregate results have passed validation and privacy review.

The material that can be prepared before results is stored elsewhere:

- `docs/experiment_protocol.md` defines the complete experiment design;
- `docs/research_basis.md` contains the literature and hardware rationale; and
- `planner_view_template.md` provides the planner review checklist.

`scripts/run_hybrid.py` generates a data-specific `planner_view.md` and detailed CSV
inside the chosen run directory. Those artifacts may contain approved challenge
identifiers and should not be committed unless the data owner approves them.

When the final artifacts are created, use only metrics generated by the common
pipeline. Do not copy solver-native objective values or raw QUBO energies into
business comparisons.
